I am a Management Accounting graduate from Ramon Magsaysay Memorial Colleges, recognized as a Dean's Lister and a 2nd Runner-Up in the Management Accounting Cup under the Junior Philippine Institute of Accountants. My academic background has equipped me with a strong analytical foundation and a detail-oriented approach to financial operations.

Currently serving as a Licensing and Regulatory Compliance Associate at Brigada Distributions, Inc., I manage the full spectrum of regulatory compliance — from business registrations and closures to permit renewals, tax payments, and government liaison across multiple branches. My role demands meticulous coordination of legal documentation, budget requests, and bank reconciliation processes, all within fast-paced, deadline-driven environments.

Prior to this, I completed an Internal Audit internship at SOCOTECO II, where I gained hands-on experience in auditing processes and internal controls. I thrive in roles that require cross-functional collaboration, multi-branch coordination, and the ability to translate complex regulatory requirements into streamlined administrative workflows.
